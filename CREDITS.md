# Credits

Every image, sound, music and font file in `assets/` must be listed here before it is committed.

## Sound effects and music

All 17 sound effects (`assets/sounds/*.wav`) and both music loops (`assets/music/menu.wav`, `assets/music/gameplay.wav`) are original. They were generated by our own synthesizer, `tools/SoundMaker.java`, so there is no third-party licence.

## Images

None yet. The game currently draws placeholder art in code (`drawFallback` in each tile and rescuer class). The planned custom sprites will be listed here when they are added.
