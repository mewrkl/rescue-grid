# Credits

Every image, sound, music and font file in `assets/` must be listed here before it is committed.

## Sound effects and music

All 21 sound effects (`assets/sounds/*.wav`) and both music loops (`assets/music/menu.wav`, `assets/music/gameplay.wav`) are original. They were generated by our own synthesizer, `tools/SoundMaker.java`, so there is no third-party licence.

## Images

All sprite sheets in `assets/images/*.png` are original. They are drawn from code by our own tool, `tools/SpriteMaker.java` (decision 0016), so there is no third-party licence. The placeholder art in code (`drawFallback`) is also ours.
